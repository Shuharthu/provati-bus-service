THE PROVATI ADVANTAGE

Why Discerning Travelers Choose Provati

From first-class sleeper berths to satellite telemetry safety tracking, we deliver airline hospitality on Bangladesh's national highways.

First-Class Sleeper Cabins

Fully flat 2x1 sleeper berths equipped with privacy curtains, sanitized beddings, and dimmable reading lamps for overnight highway tranquility.

Aviation Air Suspension

Electronically stabilized air suspension engineered to absorb highway vibrations, ensuring a smooth, floating ride over long expressways.

High-Speed Wi-Fi & USB Power

Stay powered and connected with high-speed 4G Wi-Fi routers and individual USB fast-charging ports at every reclining seat and sleeper bed.

24/7 Satellite GPS Safety

Every coach is continuously monitored by central dispatch for speed compliance, lane safety, and immediate emergency telemetry response.

Executive VIP Lounges

Enjoy air-conditioned boarding lounges in Sayedabad Central, Dampara Chattogram, and Kolatoli Beach Circle with free mineral water and tea.

Flexible 6-Hour Cash Hold

Reserve your seats online without immediate digital payment and collect tickets in cash at any counter station up to 6 hours before departure.
